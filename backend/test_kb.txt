=========================================
PERINTY SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
=========================================

What is Perinty?
Perinty is a cutting-edge platform designed to help freelancers build modern software portfolios and showcase their skills to clients on Upwork. It features tools for automations, custom dashboards, and AI RAG pipelines.

Who is the founder of Perinty?
Perinty was founded by a talented team of remote full-stack engineers in 2026 to help close the gap between software developers and remote contract opportunities.

What is the refund policy?
Perinty offers a 14-day money-back guarantee for all paid subscription plans. Refund requests must be sent directly to billing@perinty.com.

How do I connect my Stripe account?
To connect Stripe, navigate to Settings > Integrations, click the "Connect Stripe" button, and follow the Stripe Connect oauth prompts.
